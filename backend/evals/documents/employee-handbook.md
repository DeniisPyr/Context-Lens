Brightwater Labs s.r.o. and everyone named in it are invented.

## 1. About this handbook

This handbook applies to all employees of Brightwater Labs s.r.o. with an employment contract.
It does not apply to contractors, agency workers or interns on agreements outside an employment contract.
Where this handbook and your employment contract differ, your employment contract takes precedence.
Questions about this handbook should be sent to hr@brightwater.example.
HR reviews the handbook once a year and publishes changes on the intranet at hr.brightwater.example.

## 2. Working hours

### 2.1 Standard week

The standard working week is 40 hours, from Monday to Friday.
Core hours are from 10:00 to 15:00, when all office staff are expected to be available.
Outside core hours, office staff may start work between 7:00 and 10:00 and adjust the end of the day to match.
Laboratory staff work in shifts agreed with the Laboratory Operations Manager.
Every employee takes an unpaid lunch break of 30 minutes after no more than 6 hours of work.

### 2.2 Recording time

All working time is recorded in the attendance system TimeTrack before the end of each week.
Managers approve the recorded time of their team by the third working day of the following month.

### 2.3 Overtime

Overtime must be approved in advance by your line manager.
Ordered overtime may not exceed 150 hours in a calendar year.
Overtime is paid at the wage plus a 25 % supplement, or compensated with time off if both sides agree.

## 3. Remote work

### 3.1 Eligibility

Remote work is available to employees whose role does not require them to be on site every day.
Laboratory work, sample handling and instrument maintenance cannot be done remotely.
New employees may start working remotely after their first month.

### 3.2 Rules

Eligible employees may work remotely up to 2 days a week.
Remote days are agreed with the line manager in advance and recorded in TimeTrack.
Remote days cannot be carried over from one week to another.
Employees working remotely must be reachable by phone and chat during core hours.
Remote work must be done from the employee's registered home address in the Czech Republic.
Working from another country requires written approval from HR, for no more than 10 working days a year.
Company information must be accessed only over the company VPN, as described in the IT Security Policy.

### 3.3 Remote work allowance

Employees receive an allowance of CZK 35 for each full day of remote work.
The allowance covers electricity, heating and internet, and is paid with the monthly salary.
After passing probation, employees can claim a one-off contribution of up to CZK 4,000 for a monitor or chair.

## 4. Annual leave

### 4.1 Entitlement

Full-time employees are entitled to 25 days of annual leave per calendar year.
Part-time employees receive a proportional entitlement based on their contracted hours.
Employees who join or leave during the year receive a proportional part of the entitlement.

### 4.2 Requesting leave

Leave is requested in the HR portal and approved by the line manager.
Requests for 5 or more consecutive days should be made at least 4 weeks in advance.
Managers may refuse a request when it would leave the team without cover.

### 4.3 Carry-over

Employees should take their full entitlement during the calendar year.
Up to 5 days of unused annual leave may be carried over into the next calendar year.
Carried-over days must be taken by 30 June of the next year.
Unused days above this limit are forfeited unless HR approves an exception in writing.

### 4.4 Public holidays

Czech public holidays are paid days off and are not deducted from annual leave.
The office is closed on public holidays, and laboratory shifts on those days follow a separate rota.

## 5. Sick leave

### 5.1 Reporting sickness

If you are ill and cannot work, tell your line manager by 9:00 on the first day of absence.
Report the absence in the HR portal on the same day.

### 5.2 Sick days

Employees are entitled to 3 sick days per calendar year without a doctor's certificate.
Sick days are paid at the full wage and can be taken as whole or half days.
Unused sick days do not carry over into the next year.

### 5.3 Longer sickness

For longer sickness, the employee's doctor issues an electronic sick note.
For the first 14 calendar days of a sick note, the company tops up wage compensation to 80 % of average earnings.
From the 15th day, sickness benefit is paid by the state social security administration.
Employees on sick leave must follow the doctor's instructions and stay reachable at the address they gave.

## 6. Business expenses

### 6.1 Scope

This section covers business expenses other than business travel.
Business travel, including per diems and mileage, is covered by the separate Travel Expenses Policy.

### 6.2 Claims

Expenses are claimed in the expense tool Spendly with a photo or scan of the receipt.
Claims must be submitted within 30 days of the purchase.
Claims submitted later are reimbursed only with the approval of the Finance Manager.
A receipt is required for every item, whatever the amount.

### 6.3 Approval and limits

Expenses up to CZK 10,000 are approved by the line manager.
Expenses above CZK 10,000 need prior written approval from the department head.
Alcohol, fines and personal subscriptions are never reimbursed.
Approved expenses are reimbursed with the next monthly salary.

## 7. Equipment

### 7.1 Issued equipment

Every employee receives a laptop, headset and access card on their first working day.
Employees with on-call duties also receive a company mobile phone.
Laptops are replaced every 4 years, or earlier if they fail.
Reasonable personal use of company equipment is allowed if it does not affect work or security.

### 7.2 Care and loss

Employees must keep equipment in good condition and must not lend it to anyone.
Loss or theft of company equipment is handled under the IT Security Policy.
Damage caused by negligence may be charged to the employee in line with the Labour Code.

### 7.3 Return of equipment

All company equipment must be returned to the IT Service Desk on the last working day.
The access card is returned to Reception on the same day.

## 8. Probation

New employees have a probation period of 3 months.
Employees in management positions have a probation period of 6 months.
The probation period cannot be extended, except by days of full-day absence such as sickness.
Before the end of probation, the line manager holds a review meeting and records the outcome in the HR portal.
During probation, either side may end the employment at any time, with or without giving a reason.

## 9. Ending employment

### 9.1 Notice period

The notice period is 2 months for both the employee and the company.
The notice period starts on the day the written notice is delivered to the other side.
A resignation must be in writing and signed, and handed to HR or sent by registered post.
Employment can also end at any time by a written agreement between the employee and the company.

### 9.2 Leaving the company

Before the last working day, the employee completes the exit checklist in the HR portal.
Remaining annual leave is either taken during the notice period or paid out with the final salary.
HR invites every leaving employee to an exit interview.
The employment certificate is issued on the last working day.
