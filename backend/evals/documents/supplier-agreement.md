## 1. Definitions

1.1 "Services" means the application support, hosting and maintenance services described in Annex 1.
1.2 "System" means the Customer's laboratory information management system (LIMS) and its interfaces.
1.3 "Business Day" means a day other than a Saturday, a Sunday or a public holiday in the Czech Republic.
1.4 "Incident" means an unplanned interruption of the System or a reduction in its quality.
1.5 "Response Time" means the time from the Customer reporting an Incident to a qualified engineer starting work on it.
1.6 "Resolution Time" means the time from the Customer reporting an Incident to restoring the affected function.
1.7 "Monthly Fee" means the fixed fee for the Services set out in clause 5.1.

## 2. Subject of the Agreement

2.1 The Supplier shall provide the Services to the Customer in accordance with this Agreement and its Annexes.
2.2 The Customer shall pay the Supplier the price for the Services under clause 5.
2.3 The Services are provided remotely, unless on-site work is agreed for a specific Incident or change.
2.4 The Supplier shall perform the Services with professional care and in line with the GxP rules for the System.

## 3. Term

3.1 This Agreement takes effect on 1 January 2027.
3.2 This Agreement is concluded for an initial term of 3 years.
3.3 After the initial term, this Agreement renews automatically for further periods of 1 year each.
3.4 Automatic renewal does not apply if either Party notifies the other in writing that it does not wish to renew,
at least 6 months before the end of the current term.

## 4. Termination

4.1 Either Party may terminate this Agreement for convenience by giving at least 3 months' written notice.
4.2 The notice period begins on the first day of the calendar month following delivery of the notice.
4.3 Either Party may terminate this Agreement with immediate effect if the other Party commits a material breach
and does not remedy it within 15 Business Days of receiving a written request to do so.
4.4 The Customer may terminate this Agreement with immediate effect if the Supplier misses the P1 Resolution Time
more than three times in any six consecutive months.
4.5 On termination, the Supplier shall cooperate in the transfer of the Services to the Customer or a new supplier.
4.6 The Supplier shall provide exit assistance for up to 3 months after termination at the rates in Annex 2.

## 5. Price and Payment Terms

5.1 The Monthly Fee for the Services is CZK 185,000 excluding VAT.
5.2 Work outside the scope of the Services is charged at CZK 1,900 per hour excluding VAT, if approved in advance.
5.3 The Supplier shall invoice the Monthly Fee at the end of each calendar month for that month.
5.4 Invoices are payable within 30 days of their receipt by the Customer.
5.5 An invoice that does not contain the information required by law or by this Agreement may be returned,
and a new payment period begins when the corrected invoice is received.
5.6 For late payment, the Supplier may charge interest of 0.05 % of the amount due for each day of delay.
5.7 The Monthly Fee may be increased once a year, from 1 January, by the annual inflation rate published by
the Czech Statistical Office for the previous year, but by no more than 5 %.
5.8 Invoices are sent in electronic form to invoices@brightwater.example.

## 6. Service Levels

6.1 The Supplier shall meet the Response Times and Resolution Times below for each Incident priority.

| Priority | Description | Response Time | Resolution Time |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| P1 – Critical | The System is unavailable or GxP data is at risk | within 1 hour | 8 hours |
| P2 – High | A major function is unavailable and there is no workaround | within 4 hours | 2 Business Days |
| P3 – Medium | A function is impaired but a workaround exists | within 1 Business Day | 10 Business Days |
| P4 – Low | Questions, minor defects and requests for information | within 3 Business Days | Next planned release |

6.2 The priority of an Incident is set by the Customer when reporting it and may be changed only by agreement.
6.3 P1 Incidents are handled 24 hours a day, 7 days a week.
6.4 P2, P3 and P4 Incidents are handled on Business Days from 8:00 to 17:00 Central European Time.
6.5 Incidents are reported in the Supplier's service portal; P1 Incidents must also be reported by phone.
6.6 The Supplier guarantees an availability of the System of 99.5 % in each calendar month,
excluding planned maintenance announced at least 5 Business Days in advance.
6.7 For each 0.5 percentage point of availability below the guaranteed level, the Customer receives a service credit
of 5 % of the Monthly Fee.
6.8 Service credits in any calendar month are limited to 20 % of the Monthly Fee.
6.9 The Supplier shall send a service level report for each calendar month by the 10th day of the following month.
6.10 Repeated failure to meet the P1 Resolution Time gives the Customer the right to terminate under clause 4.4.
