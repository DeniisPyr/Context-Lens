## 1. What Velmorin is and what it is used for

Velmorin belongs to a group of medicines called antihistamines.
It is used to relieve the symptoms of seasonal allergic rhinitis (hay fever).
These symptoms include sneezing, a runny or blocked nose, and itchy, red or watery eyes.

## 2. What you need to know before you take Velmorin

### Do not take Velmorin

- if you are allergic to velmoradine or to any of the other ingredients of this medicine (listed in section 6)
- if you have severe liver disease

### Warnings and precautions

Talk to your doctor or pharmacist before taking Velmorin:

- if you have kidney problems
- if you have epilepsy or have ever had seizures
- if you have difficulty passing urine

### Children

Velmorin is not for children under 12 years of age.

### Other medicines and Velmorin

Tell your doctor or pharmacist if you are taking, have recently taken or might take any other medicines.
This includes medicines that make you sleepy, such as sleeping tablets and some medicines for anxiety.

### Velmorin with food, drink and alcohol

Velmorin can be taken with or without food.
Do not drink grapefruit juice while you are taking Velmorin.
Avoid alcohol, because it can make you feel more drowsy.

### Pregnancy and breast-feeding

If you are pregnant, think you may be pregnant or are planning to have a baby, do not take Velmorin.
Velmorin passes into breast milk, so do not take it while you are breast-feeding.
Ask your doctor or pharmacist for advice before taking any medicine.

### Driving and using machines

Velmorin may make you feel drowsy.
Do not drive or use machines until you know how Velmorin affects you.

### Velmorin contains lactose

If you have been told by your doctor that you cannot tolerate some sugars, ask your doctor before taking it.

## 3. How to take Velmorin

Always take this medicine exactly as your doctor or pharmacist has told you.
Check with your doctor or pharmacist if you are not sure.

### Adults and adolescents aged 12 years and over

The recommended dose is one 20 mg tablet once a day.
Do not take more than one tablet in 24 hours.

### Patients with kidney problems

If you have severe kidney problems, the recommended dose is one 20 mg tablet every other day.
Your doctor may check how well your kidneys work before you start treatment.

### How to take it

Swallow the tablet whole with a glass of water.
Do not crush or chew the tablet.
Try to take the tablet at the same time every day.

### If you take more Velmorin than you should

If you take more Velmorin than you should, contact your doctor or the nearest hospital emergency department at once.
Take the medicine pack and this leaflet with you.
Symptoms of an overdose may include severe drowsiness, confusion and a fast or irregular heartbeat.

### If you forget to take Velmorin

If you forget a dose, take it as soon as you remember, unless it is more than 12 hours after the usual time.
In that case, skip the missed dose and take the next dose at the usual time.
Do not take a double dose to make up for a forgotten tablet.

## 4. Possible side effects

Like all medicines, this medicine can cause side effects, although not everybody gets them.

### Stop taking Velmorin and get medical help immediately if you notice

- swelling of the face, lips, tongue or throat, which may cause difficulty in swallowing or breathing
- a severe skin rash with blisters or peeling skin

### Other side effects

Very common (may affect more than 1 in 10 people):

- drowsiness

Common (may affect up to 1 in 10 people):

- headache
- dry mouth
- tiredness

Uncommon (may affect up to 1 in 100 people):

- dizziness
- nausea
- stomach pain

Rare (may affect up to 1 in 1,000 people):

- fast heartbeat
- itchy skin rash
- changes in blood tests of liver function

## 5. How to store Velmorin

Keep this medicine out of the sight and reach of children.
Do not store above 25 °C.
Store in the original package in order to protect from moisture.
Do not use this medicine after the expiry date which is stated on the carton after EXP.
Do not throw away any medicines via wastewater or household waste; ask your pharmacist how to dispose of them.
